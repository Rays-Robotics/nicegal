Nicegal app icon

Magnifier: Search Icon.svg by Jayprakash12345, with alignment and handle
edits by KCVelaga, sourced from:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Search_Icon.svg
Wikimedia identifies this simple geometric artwork as public domain.
The magnifier's original paths, transforms, and dark-red color are preserved.

The Yosho sprite was restored from the user-supplied image to a 16x16 grid
with flat colors. The magnifier's public-domain status does not apply to
the supplied character.

build/icon.svg is the editable master. Its square viewBox crops the original
500x500 canvas to (36,36)-(464,464), retaining approximately 4% vertical
padding. The lens is white and the exterior transparent.

The final Search Icon.svg choice supersedes the earlier Vector search
icon.svg, Lucide, and Noto choices. Per the user's subsequent cleanup
instruction, resources/licenses and resources/icon.png remain; only
resources/icon-design was removed.

PNG export:
rsvg-convert -w 1024 -h 1024 -o build/icon.png build/icon.svg
Copy build/icon.png to resources/icon.png for the running app.

Windows icon export:
magick build/icon.png -define icon:auto-resize=256,128,64,48,32,24,16 build/icon.ico

The ICNS file contains PNG representations at 128, 256, 512, and 1024 pixels.
